Core features

Built around your privacy & continuity

Every feature is designed with patient control at the centre.

Lifelong Timeline

Every lab report, prescription, scan, and clinical note in a single chronological timeline — across every hospital and clinic you've ever visited.

Granular Consent

Decide exactly which records each provider can view. Set time-limited access windows, or revoke instantly. You remain the custodian of your data.

ABDM Architecture

Built around the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission framework. Designed for interoperability with India's emerging federated health data network.

AI Health Assistant

Ask questions about your reports in plain English. Understand what your test values mean, get plain-language summaries of your health history.

Universal Health QR

A scannable QR code that identifies you to any HealthVault-enabled provider. Share your full vault or select records — one scan, instant access.

Emergency Card

Critical information — blood group, allergies, conditions, emergency contacts — always accessible, even without phone unlock or app login.

For providers

A unified view of every patient

Doctors, hospitals, and labs get a purpose-built portal — no paper chase, no missed history.

  • View complete patient timelines with consent-gated access
  • Add clinical notes, prescriptions, and encounter summaries
  • Labs upload reports directly to a patient's vault
  • Request-based access — never unauthorized data pull
  • Full audit trail of every access event
